# Spine contributors

Spine builds on the work of the Cacti Group and the authors credited in its
source notices. Those credits remain in the maintained source files:

- Larry Adams: development and enhancements.
- Rivo Nurges: RRD support, MySQL poller cache and miscellaneous functions.
- RTG: core poller code, pthreads, SNMP and Autoconf examples.
- Brady Alleman and Doug Warner: threading ideas and implementation details.
- Xenadyne Inc., the Regents of the University of California and Ken Arnold:
  the process implementation's inherited code and notices.

Additional major contributors identified from the repository's Git history
include Ian Berry, Jimmy Conner (`cigamit`), Tony Roman, Mark Brugnoli-Vinten
(`netniV`), `eriksejr`, Reinhard Scheck and Jeff Pasnak (`Linegod`).
Larry Adams also appears as `TheWitness` in that history.

Thomas Vincent maintains this fork and contributed its C17 modernization,
module boundaries, regression coverage, CI and security tooling.

This list records project contributions, not copyright assignments or authorship
of every file. It is not exhaustive; Git history and preserved source notices
remain the detailed attribution record. Contributions are welcome with their
existing authorship and licensing information intact.
